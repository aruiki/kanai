Asteria in use.

Type while AI prepares.

The normal AI path publishes candidates after the first generation and scoring result. Reading display, editing and cancellation remain available while models prepare asynchronously. Candidate arrival time depends on the PC, reading and model state.

If AI is disabled or unavailable, Mozc maintains input.

Try it on your PC.

Windows 10 1809 or later / Windows 11 x64, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), about 1.7 GB disk space. GPU AI uses a dedicated Vulkan GPU with at least 3 GiB VRAM. PCs without one use CPU-oriented Low, with different quality and speed. Integrated GPUs are not used.

Free and open source. This release candidate is unsigned.

Evidence, with its conditions.

A fixed offline development evaluation scored 188/200 (94.0%) on AJIMEE-Bench using Standard, an RTX 3060, warmed AI and supplied preceding context. Without context: 175/200 (87.5%). These are tuning/regression measurements, not installed-IME app accuracy or a current comparison with other IMEs.

The RC4 MSI passed installation, input, uninstall and eight candidate operations in EDIT/RichEdit. Installed TSF GPU AI contribution, multiple apps, low-end hardware, protected-field internals, upgrades from an older release, reboot and independent product completion remain unverified.
